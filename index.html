A simple watch guide

A few things to know about watches.

Whether you are comparing automatic and quartz watches or looking for practical watch-care tips, start with the details that matter to your daily routine.

What is an automatic watch?

An automatic watch is a mechanical wristwatch that uses the wearer's wrist movement to wind its mainspring. Unlike a quartz watch, it does not rely on a battery for its timekeeping mechanism. Accuracy and power reserve vary by movement.

How is a mechanical watch different from a quartz watch?

A mechanical watch keeps time using a spring-driven movement with small moving parts. A quartz watch uses a battery and a quartz crystal to regulate time. Each type has different maintenance needs, features, and appeal.

How should I care for a wristwatch?

Follow the maker's instructions for cleaning, servicing, and water exposure. Avoid strong magnetic fields unless your watch is rated to resist them, and have mechanical watch servicing handled by a qualified professional.
